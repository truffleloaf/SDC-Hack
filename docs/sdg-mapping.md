# SDG mapping

These are the team’s judgments, not an official UN classification or a claim of measured impact. The official goal names and colours follow the UN SDG communication catalogue: https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/news/communications-material/ .

Canonical data: `backend/app/data/sdg-map.json`. `lib/data/sdg-map.json` is the identical generated frontend artifact. Every skill includes education (4); additional tags group technical/analytical practice with innovation (9), operational work with decent work (8), and communication with inclusion (10). Role tags retain the documented catalogue assignments. Optional focus adds 5 score points per matching goal, capped at 10; the original score and rank remain visible. Counts show mapped goals, not verified social outcomes.
